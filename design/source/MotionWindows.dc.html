Window opensThe new window pops in at its tile from 86 % with a slight overshoot (Hyprland "popin 86%", 280 ms); the existing window slides to make room. Closing (end of loop) shrinks to 90 % and fades.
Window closes · Super+QThe window shrinks to 90 % and fades (200 ms) while its neighbour grows into the space; focus moves to the neighbour and its accent border fades in.
Switch workspace · Super+1–9Workspaces slide horizontally (left for a higher number, right for lower, 300 ms). The pills stay put; only the capsule in the workspace pill stretches along with the slide.
Overview · Super+TabThe current workspace zooms out into its card (380 ms) while the others fade in around it and the background blurs. Enter on another card zooms into that one instead.
Alt+TabAppears only if Alt is held longer than 120 ms (a quick Alt+Tab just swaps windows without showing it). The ring glides between icons; releasing Alt fades it and focuses the window.
Fullscreen · Super+F and gamesThe window grows to the edges (260 ms) and the pills float up and fade. Toasts are held back until you leave fullscreen, except critical ones.
